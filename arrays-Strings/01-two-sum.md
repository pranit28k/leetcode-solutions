## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

I used a simple nested-loop approach. For every element, I checked the remaining elements to find two numbers whose sum is equal to the target.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

The solution returns the indices of the two numbers whose sum is equal to the target.
